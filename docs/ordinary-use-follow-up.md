# Ordinary-use follow-up, 4 November 2026

The review uses read-only diagnostics and the post-28-October ordinary-use cohort.
Personal transcripts and raw diagnostic snapshots remain in ignored local scratch;
they are not evaluation fixtures or repository documentation.

## Repeated, underspecified forward questions

The forward-curiosity producer retained a topic in its pool payload but rendered
only a model-drafted question. A vague draft therefore lost its referent. The
wants feeder independently copied historical journal questions, and initiative
could repeatedly deliver them without claiming the owning pool cue. Its lexical
acted-on test could miss an otherwise relevant answer to the vague wording.

Forward cues now retain the original activity, source timestamp and topic in the
rendered context. The handling section requires a specific question and one
clarification when the source itself is vague. Wants read available pool rows,
including migration of old journal references, and claim the same cue through
the final-prompt staging contract. Alternative delivery routes carry their cue's
handling as a budgeted bundle. Awaiting, resolved, expired and delayed questions
cannot survive in an independent wants list. Forward questions have one verified
ask; an unrelated response is not recorded as successful fulfillment.

Validation includes isolated producer/wants/final-prompt/ask/answer chains,
omission without consumption, unrelated replies, legacy references and ordinary
cue/initiative/handling regressions. These checks establish the delivery contract,
not guaranteed model adherence on every live turn. Backend restart is needed.

## 1. Model-output contracts

An isolated synthetic probe on the configured Venice chat route reproduced the
second-thought failure: all 160 completion tokens were hidden reasoning, with
zero visible characters and a length stop. Disabling reasoning on that call
produced a valid two-field draft using 80 visible tokens. Second thought now
overrides effort only for this provider, retaining the configured output cap,
cache affinity and other providers' effort settings. Truncated drafts are never
queued, even when partial fields parse; content-free output metrics distinguish
truncation from malformed text and legitimate NONE declines.

Situation extraction keeps rejecting unknown citations. Feedback on the next
already-scheduled call now covers the observed nested evidence_id and
evidence_shape reasons as well as top-level IDs and missing user evidence. No
extra inference retry or guessed replacement citation is introduced.

## 2. Source-grounded concept review

`python scripts/review_concept_cohort.py --since YYYY-MM-DD --output <private.json>`
exports a deterministic round-robin sample by subject/kind/status with separate
claims, rationales and direct evidence. The recorded cutoff and seed allow the
cohort selection to be reproduced. Cluster evidence uses representative memory
IDs, not database cluster IDs; cached cluster names are explicitly not historical
membership proof. Original source messages retain their speaker attribution.

Optional annotations report judged denominators for support, distinctness, scope
and register. Unreviewed items remain unknown. Balanced sample rates do not
estimate the prevalence of errors in the full graph.

The ordinary-use review identified concrete cases of repetition interpreted as
development and compatible preferences interpreted as conflict. One rationale
explicitly added an unstated assumption about minimizing words. These are useful
held-out contrast families for the next proposer evaluation. No existing concept,
threshold or personal memory was automatically rewritten from this small review.
L22's general model-quality acceptance remains open.

## 3. Cue-delivery baseline and alternative routes

`python scripts/cue_delivery_report.py --since YYYY-MM-DD` joins content-free
final assembly claims, cue decisions and retained ask/feedback receipts. Retry
attempts do not count as final delivery. Missing receipts remain unknown, and
stage counters explicitly have different units rather than forming a conversion
funnel or a usefulness score.

The first post-rollout baseline had 180 measured turns and 128 retained final
traces; 52 older turns were outside trace retention. It showed both omitted
optional claims and committed cues without verified ask receipts. Historical
block reach therefore cannot establish that an answerable question was asked.

G4 accounting now reads final committed claims as well as canonical block names.
A forward question delivered through wants/initiative is attributed to its real
cue family. Omitted/retried/failed claims do not count as delivery, and an
alternative route is not treated as winning the separate gap-cue mutex.
No topic gate or cooldown was relaxed from aggregate rates.

# Further worker quality improvements

Implementation of the [6 Nov worker audit](worker-quality-audit-2026-11-06.md).
The existing stores remain authoritative; historical records are not rewritten.

## Internal growth reflections

Mood and relationship-signal changes describe Aiko's recorded experience, with
explicit baseline and later-window dates. They do not establish user growth,
causes, or a change since first meeting. Aiko goals are not used as corroboration
of the user's work. Delivery uses the cue pool with a single-item inventory,
expiry, freshness checks and consumption accounting. The old journal is retained
as diagnostics; legacy ring entries cannot surface through this provider.

Focused growth/policy checks: 136 tests and 93 subtests passed. Live phrasing is
an ordinary-use check after restart, not established by these structural tests.

## Background diary and thread inputs

Transcript packing keeps a contiguous suffix of whole messages. A final negation
or imagined-scene qualification survives intact, or the message is omitted whole.
The background diary freezes the source messages, user name and session before
generation, records continuity inputs as subjective derivation, and rechecks its
sources after generation and embedding. A session switch cannot relabel the old
draft as a new session's experience. Thread resummary uses the same packing with
its existing history-edit save guard. Post-sleep diary output is accepted whole
or rejected, and retains the session captured before generation.

Regression coverage includes source edits at both generation boundaries, session
switches, long-message final negation and overlong sleep-diary output. The combined
focused worker set passed 242 tests and 6 subtests; lint passed.

## Research support and question revisions

Research receipts separate all offered URLs from the excerpts selected as support.
Lineage reads selected support even on historical receipts that still list all
offered URLs. Missing citations no longer silently become support from the first
result; runtime workers abstain and report unattributed output. Overlong distilled
facts are rejected whole rather than shortened past an exception.

Curiosity freezes its question before search/generation, rechecks it before and
after embedding, and resolves only that unchanged question. Edits also prevent an
old failed attempt from marking a different question inconclusive. These receipts
establish attribution, not independent verification or entailment of the claim.

Focused regressions cover selected-source lineage, uncited refusals, overlong
facts and question edits during model generation and embedding.

## Historical evolution diary

The evolution diary remains its own append-only change log. Composition receives
the original event dates, their historical period, and distinct user, Aiko,
relationship and world subjects. A relationship change is no longer presented as
a claim only about the user. The prompt anchors durable dates and past changes;
relative or overlong output is rejected without consuming event history. Existing
event links, period bounds and watermark behavior are preserved.

Focused tests cover old relationship events in the prompt and rejected prose
leaving the historical events available for a later pass.

## Qualified knowledge-map reflections

Map composition receives whole, bounded concept records with subjects, rationale,
status, dates and the existing evidence projection's applicability, uncertainty,
source modes/domains and revisions. Incomplete inspection excerpts are not fed
back as quotations. Equal wording cannot swap a selected concept's subject.
Oversized records are omitted whole. The projection remains a read through
ConceptView; no alternative concept store or confidence-voting scheme is added.

Memory counts and last use are labelled as storage/recall activity; sparse public
research coverage does not establish a gap in personal understanding. A stable
context signature ignores recall counts, changing age labels, confidence drift
and ordering. It prevents a timer from creating another paraphrase of unchanged
understanding. A real source or scope change invalidates an in-flight draft.
Empty or unsafe completions spend the unchanged context; transient model failures
remain retryable. Saved derivation is generation context, not new observations.

Focused checks cover subject collisions, conditions, virtual scope, changed inputs
during model/embedding calls, unchanged context and whole-output rejection.
